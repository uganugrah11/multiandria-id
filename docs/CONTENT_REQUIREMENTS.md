# Content Requirements — Multi Andria Indonesia

All items below are things the new website will need but that could **not** be verified from the current live site or provided instructions. None of these have been invented or assumed anywhere in the discovery/design documents — they are explicitly marked `CONTENT NEEDED` at the point of use, and listed here centrally for the business to action.

## Business Identity & Contact
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Confirmed WhatsApp Business number and preferred default greeting message (for `config('company.whatsapp')`).
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Official logo files (vector/high-res) and any existing brand color guideline, to ground the palette decision in `DESIGN_DIRECTION.md` rather than proposing directions from scratch.
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Clarification of the relationship (if any) between PT Multi Andria Indonesia and PT Andria Fesyen Indonesia Tekstil (see `DISCOVERY.md` §1.8) — affects what content can legitimately be attributed to MAI.
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Social media account links (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.), if any, for footer.
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Company profile PDF/deck, if one exists, for a "Download Company Profile" type asset (used by the AFIT reference site).

## Manufacturing Capability
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Does MAI offer CMT (Cut-Make-Trim) and/or FOB (Free on Board) service models? (Both reference sites offer these; not confirmed for MAI.)
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Exact current production capacity figure to publish (the only verified figure is "up to 5,000 pcs/day" as of 2024 — confirm if still current).
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Machine/line counts (sewing machines, cutting tables, embroidery machines, printing units) if the business wants to publish specific capability numbers similar to the Akarsa reference. Do not invent these.
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Standard lead time(s) by product type or order size.
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Certifications (ISO, export certifications, etc.), if any.
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Confirmed current factory square footage/locations beyond the verified 2020 figure (1,860 m², Sukabumi) and the 2023 Bintaro HQ expansion (4-story building, size unconfirmed).
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Whether factory visits/surveys are offered to prospective clients (a strong FAQ answer on the Akarsa reference — not confirmed for MAI).
- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Payment terms/scheme (DP percentage, payment tempo options, etc.).

## Products

- `CONTENT NEEDED`: Whether MOQ and pricing figures currently shown are accurate/current and approved for public display (pricing display strategy should also be confirmed given instructions' "do not make price the dominant visual element" guidance).
