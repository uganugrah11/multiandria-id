## Method note

`public/company_profile.pdf` is 27.6 MB, larger than this session's direct-read limit, and page-image rendering (`pdftoppm`) wasn't available in this environment — so it was read via `pdftotext -layout`, which extracts the embedded text layer only. Anything presented in the PDF purely as a graphic (the "Pencapaian" stats page, most of the "Contoh Hasil Produksi" photo captions' surrounding imagery, the client logo walls) could be read as text where it appeared as text (e.g., the production-example captions), but the actual photographs and the Pencapaian infographic's numbers could not be verified this way. Those gaps are called out explicitly above and in `CONTENT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, rather than guessed at.
